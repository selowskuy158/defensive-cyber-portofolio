# A10 - Discover Privacy Technique Used Offline

**Date:** 31 March 2026
**Location:** Home and public spaces, Perth, Western Australia

## Summary
Explored how privacy can be protected offline in everyday life through physical habits and simple personal practices.

## Privacy Techniques Found

### 1. Secure Storage of Personal Documents and ID Items
- Important documents such as passport, bank papers, and identity cards are kept in a secure place at home instead of being left in open areas.
- This reduces the chance of other people seeing, copying, or taking personal information.
- I observed that keeping these items in one private storage area helps protect sensitive details such as address, date of birth, and account information.

### 2. Safe Disposal of Documents with Personal Information
- Papers containing personal information should not be thrown away openly where others can read them.
- Tearing up or shredding documents before disposal helps prevent information exposure.
- I observed this as an easy offline privacy habit because printed documents can still leak private information even without any internet use.

### 3. Preventing Shoulder-Surfing in Shared Spaces
- Privacy can also be protected by making sure personal information is not visible to other people in public or shared places.
- This includes being careful when reading messages, entering PIN numbers, or viewing private documents near other people.
- I noticed that even simple actions like adjusting screen position or covering information while entering details can help keep private data safe offline.
