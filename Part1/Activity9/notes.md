# A9 - Discover Privacy Technique Used Online

**Date:** 22 March, 2026

## Summary
Explored online privacy techniques that protect user data and browsing activity on the internet.

## Privacy Techniques Found

### 1. Cookie Consent Banners (GDPR Compliance)
- Websites show popup banners asking users to accept or reject tracking cookies
- Required by GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) from the EU
- Tested on BBC, Amazon, Reddit - all showed cookie management options
- Rejecting non-essential cookies prevents websites from tracking browsing behavior

### 2. End-to-End Encryption in WhatsApp
- Messages encrypted on sender device, only decrypted on receiver device
- Even WhatsApp cannot read the message content
- Verified by checking chat info showing "Messages and calls are end-to-end encrypted"

### 3. DuckDuckGo Private Search Engine
- Does not track search queries or store personal information
- No tracking cookies used to follow users across websites
- Compared with Google - Google showed targeted ads after searching, DuckDuckGo did not
