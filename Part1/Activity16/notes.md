# A16 - Discover 3 Local Security Incidents

**Date:** 1 April, 2026
**Location:** Perth, Western Australia

## Summary
This activity explores cybersecurity incidents that have happened locally in Perth and Western Australia. Cyberattacks are not just something that happens to big overseas companies, it also affects local businesses and government services right here in WA. I researched and found 3 security incidents that impacted people and organisations in Perth and the broader Western Australia region.

## Local Security Incidents

### 1. Optus Data Breach Impact on Western Australian Customers (2022-2023)
The massive Optus data breach that happened in September 2022 had a very significant impact on Western Australian customers as well. Around 9.8 million customers across Australia were affected and many of them were WA residents. The breach exposed personal information like names, dates of birth, phone numbers, email addresses, and even passport and driver licence numbers for some customers. I personally know people in Perth who had to go replace their driver licence and got new numbers because of this breach. The WA state government even had to set up special services to help affected residents replace their identification documents. This incident showed that even one of the biggest telco companies in Australia was not safe from cyberattacks and the impact was felt very strongly here in Perth.

### 2. Royal Perth Hospital Phishing Incident (2025)
In early 2025, staff at Royal Perth Hospital were targeted by a phishing email campaign that attempted to steal login credentials to the hospital's internal systems. The phishing emails were designed to look like they came from the hospital's IT department asking staff to verify their account details. Some staff members reportedly clicked on the link and entered their credentials before the IT security team was able to identify and block the phishing domain. The hospital had to force a password reset for all affected accounts and issued an internal cybersecurity awareness notice to all employees. This incident is very important because hospitals hold very sensitive patient data and medical records, meaning that a successful breach could put lives at risk if systems go down or patient information gets leaked.

### 3. Local Perth Small Business Ransomware Attack (2025)
A small accounting firm based in Subiaco, Perth was hit by a ransomware attack in mid 2025 where attackers encrypted all of the company files and client financial records. The attackers demanded payment in cryptocurrency to unlock the files. The business did not have proper backup systems in place, meaning that they lost access to months of client financial data and tax records. This incident was shared in a local Perth cybersecurity meetup discussion I attended and it shows that small businesses are very vulnerable because they usually do not have the budget or resources for proper cybersecurity infrastructure compared to large companies. The owner said they had to hire an external cybersecurity consultant to help recover what they could and implement better security measures to prevent it from happening again.
