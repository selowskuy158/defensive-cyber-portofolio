# B9 - Participate in an Industry-Related Cybersecurity Event

**Date:** 10 April, 2026

## Summary
This activity involves participating in an industry-related cybersecurity event to gain exposure to the professional cybersecurity community and learn about current industry practices and trends. I participated in a local cybersecurity networking event in Perth that brought together professionals, students, and vendors from the cybersecurity industry.

## Event Details

### Perth Cybersecurity Networking Night
- **Date:** 28 March, 2026
- **Location:** Spacecubed, Perth CBD
- **Organiser:** AustCyber WA (Western Australian Cyber Security Innovation Node)
- **Attendees:** Approximately 50-60 people including cybersecurity professionals, university students, and technology vendors

### My Experience
I attended the Perth Cybersecurity Networking Night which was a casual evening event where cybersecurity professionals and students could meet, share ideas, and learn about the latest trends in the industry. The event started with a short keynote presentation about the state of cybersecurity in Western Australia, focusing on how WA businesses are increasingly being targeted by cybercriminals and the growing demand for cybersecurity professionals in the region.

After the keynote, there was a networking session where I had the opportunity to speak with several cybersecurity professionals working in different areas including penetration testing, security operations, and governance risk and compliance. One professional I spoke with was a SOC analyst at a Perth mining company who explained how their team monitors security events 24/7 using a SIEM platform and how they respond to potential threats in real time. Another person I met was a cybersecurity consultant who specialised in helping small businesses in Perth improve their security posture.

The event also had a small vendor exhibition area where companies showcased their cybersecurity products and services. I was particularly interested in a demonstration of an endpoint detection and response platform that used machine learning to identify and block suspicious behavior on employee laptops.

Overall, this event gave me a much better understanding of the cybersecurity industry in Perth and helped me see the different career paths available in this field. It also showed me that the cybersecurity community in Perth is very welcoming and supportive of students who are interested in entering the field.
