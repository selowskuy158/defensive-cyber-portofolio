======================================================================
  RECOMMENDATIONS
======================================================================

  [CRITICAL] Change all breached passwords immediately.
  Breached passwords are actively used in credential-stuffing attacks.

  [HIGH] Replace all WEAK passwords with randomly generated ones.
  Use a password manager (Bitwarden is free) to generate and store
  unique 16+ character passwords for every account.

  [BEST PRACTICE] Enable 2FA/MFA on all accounts that support it.
  Even a strong password is insufficient if the site gets breached
  and the hash is cracked — 2FA adds an independent second factor.

  [BEST PRACTICE] Use a passphrase (4+ random words) for passwords
  you need to type manually (master password, device unlock).
  Passphrases are easier to remember and harder to crack than short
  complex strings.
