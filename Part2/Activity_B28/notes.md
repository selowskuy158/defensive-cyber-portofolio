# B28 - Produce a Cyber Safety Flyer for University Students

**Date:** 18 April, 2026

## Summary
This activity involves producing a cyber safety flyer targeted at university students. I designed and created a one-page cyber safety flyer using Canva that provides practical tips and advice for UWA students on how to protect themselves from common cyber threats that specifically affect university students.

## Flyer Details

### Target Audience
The flyer is designed specifically for university students at UWA who may not have much cybersecurity knowledge. The language is kept simple and casual, and the tips are focused on threats that students are most likely to encounter in their daily lives as university students.

### Content of the Flyer

#### Title: "Stay Cyber Safe at UWA - A Quick Guide for Students"

The flyer covers 6 key cybersecurity tips for students:

1. **Use Strong Unique Passwords** - Do not use the same password for your UWA account, email, banking, and social media. Use a password manager like Bitwarden which is free for students.

2. **Enable Two-Factor Authentication** - Turn on 2FA on your UWA Pheme account, email, and social media. Even if someone steals your password, they cannot get in without the second factor.

3. **Be Careful on Public WiFi** - The free WiFi at cafes and libraries can be risky. Avoid logging into banking or sensitive accounts on public WiFi. Use a VPN if you need to.

4. **Watch Out for Phishing Emails** - UWA students are targeted by fake emails pretending to be from IT support, student services, or scholarship offers. Always check the sender email address carefully before clicking any links.

5. **Lock Your Devices** - Always lock your laptop and phone when you leave them unattended in the library or lecture hall. Set up a strong PIN or biometric lock.

6. **Keep Your Software Updated** - Regularly update your operating system, browser, and apps to patch security vulnerabilities that attackers can exploit.

### Design
The flyer uses a clean modern design with the UWA colour scheme of blue and gold. Each tip has a simple icon next to it for easy visual identification. The bottom of the flyer includes the UWA IT Help Desk contact information and a QR code linking to the UWA cybersecurity resources page. The flyer was designed using Canva and exported as a PDF file which was uploaded to my GitHub repository.
